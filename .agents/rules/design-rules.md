# Permanent Design Constraints for EasyWindmill

All future edits must follow these constraints:
1. Never use purple gradients.
2. Never use pill-shaped buttons (`999px`). Use clean `4px` to `8px` rectangular radius.
3. No fake reviews, star ratings, or artificial metrics.
4. No vague hero text; use precise, grounded engineering language.
5. No emoji icons anywhere in UI or copy.
6. No em dashes (`—`) or en dashes (`–`); use hyphens or clear punctuation.
7. No excessive scroll animations.
8. Remove any "Made with AI" tags or references.
9. Must maintain active Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions pages.
10. No AI slop photos or fake customer counters; use authentic CAD frames or blueprint schematics.
